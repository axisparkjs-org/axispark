The health checks can cache their results: new `cacheTtl` option of `@HealthCheck()` and of `HealthEngine.register()`, and `healthChecks.cacheTtl` in the application config for every check, so frequent probes do not hit the databases and brokers continuously. Concurrent health requests share the checks that are running.
