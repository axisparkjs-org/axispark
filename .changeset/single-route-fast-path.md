`HttpEngine` skips the host and version matching for a route that is the only one of its method and path, accepts every host and has no versions (versioning disabled), which is the most common case. `context.host` and `context.version` are `undefined` for those requests, so `HostGuard` and `VersionGuard` have nothing to check.
