The host patterns of the routes are normalized, split and scored once, when the routes are registered, instead of on every request. New `HostPattern` (exported by `@axisparkjs/http`) and `RouteDefinition.hostPatterns` / `RouteDefinition.hostSpecificity`; the request host is normalized and split once, in `HostDefinition.setHost()`.
