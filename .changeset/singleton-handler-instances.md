`HandlerInvoker` keeps the instances of the singleton middlewares, guards, interceptors, filters and handlers after their first resolution, so each execution only resolves the scoped and transient targets with the injector. New `Injector.isSingleton(token)` and `Container.isSingleton(token)`, which check whether a token is bound to a singleton or a value, also through `useExisting` aliases.
